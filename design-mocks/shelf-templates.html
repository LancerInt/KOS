Amazon USA — Shelf, three refinements

Standalone design sheet · same six sample products across three shelf treatments · nothing here is wired to KOS.
01

Warm Catalog

The refined original: rounded product cards on a wooden shelf, a marigold thumbnail, and the 12‑pip readiness strip (one pip per selling section). Warm, browseable, closest to the app's card language.

02

Boutique

Editorial and premium. Taller duotone hero, larger display type, readiness as a five‑star rating with a "sections ready" caption, and a price‑tag record count. Square corners and a hairline shelf give it a boutique feel.

03

Aisle

Dense and scannable — four to a row, each on a continuous shelf rail. Compact cards with a thin readiness bar and a status dot. Best when a workspace holds many projects and you're skimming the aisle.

Palette & type match KOS "Ink & Flow" (brand teal, one coral for attention) with a marigold "storefront" accent for Amazon USA. Fonts: Manrope · IBM Plex Sans · IBM Plex Mono.
